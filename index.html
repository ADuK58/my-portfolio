Internal Security Policy

Neural Lab operates as an independent decentralized research collective. We apply rigorous architectural security frameworks to preserve user privacy and data integrity.

Data Minimums: Since our systems strictly discard form input integrations, our nodes do not store, compile, or process persistent personal datasets. Communication is kept restricted to direct cryptographic client-to-node email transfers.

Network Logging: Standard cryptographic key exchanges, handshake timestamps, and general analytical logs are temporarily recorded solely to prevent malicious access, DDoS events, and to audit infrastructure latency.

Retention & Encryption: All exchange logs remain strongly encrypted within private isolated Docker blocks behind reverse-proxy firewalls. We do not maintain long-term consumer databases, sell records, or engage with third-party analytical advertising systems.

Revocation: Users retain complete control over their session artifacts. To permanently purge log arrays or communication trails, contact us at: inquiries@neurallab-ai.io.
